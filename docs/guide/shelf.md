# Karots Shelf

**Karots Shelf** (full name Karots Suite Shelf) is an Android app for your own staff, for the weeks
when the shop is being set up. Each person walks the shelves with their phone and puts products
into Karots Suite one at a time:

- scan the barcode with the camera,
- read the name, price or expiry off the label with the camera instead of typing,
- ✨ Identify, when the shop has AI Assistant,
- set the category, unit, supplier, cost and selling prices, packs, and the stock already on the
  shelf (with its batches and expiry dates).

Several people can work at once. Everything goes straight into the shop, under each person's
own name in Activity.

## Who can use it

- **Admins and managers** can always use it.
- **Cashiers and sales people** only when you switch on **Can use Karots Shelf** for them on
  **Setup → Users**.

Shelf can do everything above for anyone who has it: it doesn't look at their other rights (such
as seeing cost prices). When setup is done, **switch it off again** for those people. A phone that
is already signed in stops working for them the next time it reaches the shop.

Signing in to Shelf doesn't take anyone off their till: a cashier can be selling at the counter
and adding products on their phone at the same time.

## Getting it and signing in

The Karots team installs Karots Shelf on your staff's phones. The first time, it asks for the
shop's address and then the person's **phone number and PIN**, the same ones they use at the till.
The phone must be on the shop's Wi-Fi for that first sign-in.

## Without the network

After the first sign-in Shelf works anywhere in the shop, even where the Wi-Fi doesn't reach.
New and changed products wait on the phone (**Waiting to push**) and go into the shop by
themselves when the phone is back in reach. **Pull** brings in what everyone else has added.

If the shop says no to something (for example, someone else already added that barcode), it stays
in **Waiting to push** with the reason, so the person can fix it.

The app locks itself after a few idle minutes; the person's PIN opens it again, offline too.

## Stock already on the shelf

Stock entered from Shelf goes in through **stock intake**, so **Stock-take** must be on in
**Settings**. If it's off, Shelf asks the person to ask the owner to turn it on.
