Karots Suite agreement · 12 points for a Sri Lankan attorney-at-law

Karots sells Karots Suite, a till and shop-management program, to small Sri Lankan shops for a one-time price. Each shop signs a Licence and Service Agreement in the usual Sri Lankan form (parties, recitals, terms, attestation with two witnesses, then Schedules, the First being the Order Details), which brings in the Shop Rules & Service Guide. We wrote them ourselves from the statutes listed below. Please check each point, write your opinion and any change, and tick a decision.
