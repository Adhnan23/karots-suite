2 · Free in year 1 when done remotely

Done by phone, WhatsApp or a remote session (RustDesk or similar, only when you let us in):

  • How-to questions and help with problems.
  • Bringing your data back from a backup (yours or Karots cloud).
  • Resetting the owner's PIN when every admin is locked out.
  • Moving the shop to a new computer.
  • Setting up a receipt or label printer, barcode scanner or cash drawer.
  • Training a new cashier or manager.

If you ask us to come to the shop for any of these, it's a visit: one of your 2 free visits in year 1, or the visit fee by distance.

3 · Personalised help

In the first 60 days we help you set the shop up your way at no charge: your categories, menus, prices, receipts, staff and how you work. After 60 days this kind of help is charged by how complicated the job is. We tell you the price first, and nothing is done until you agree.

4 · Always charged

  • Problems the shop caused: deleted data, a virus, a broken, wiped or replaced computer, settings changed by someone, damage from power cuts. We fix them at the normal rate.
  • New features or changes made only for your shop, and new plugins added later (see 6).
  • Typing in your products (catalogue entry) or bringing in data from another system, unless agreed on the Order Form.
  • Hardware: computers, printers, scanners, drawers, UPS, internet. We can advise, but repairs and parts are not ours.
  • Visits beyond the 2 free ones, and all visits after year 1.
  • Anything after year 1, unless you renew.

Paid work is quoted before we start, and paid when the job is done unless the quote says otherwise.

5 · Karots cloud backup (paid service)

  • A copy of your newest backup is sent to Karots' cloud about once a day, encrypted with a key for your shop only.
  • Paid in advance, monthly or yearly.
  • If a payment is more than 7 days late, the service is frozen: no new copies are sent. Copies already stored stay until their keeping time ends. Pay, and it starts again.
  • Freezing never stops your till, and the backups on your own PC and USB carry on as normal.
  • You can switch it off yourself on the Data page. Months already paid are not refunded.

6 · Plugins

  • The plugins you start with and their prices are agreed on the Order Form.
  • Adding a plugin later: the price on the Price List at the time. We set it up remotely for free.
  • Removing a plugin follows the table in 7, counted from the day that plugin was installed. Its records stay in your backups.
  • Swapping a plugin within 14 days: you pay or get back the difference in price.

8 · Shop rules: please do

  • Give every person their own login and keep PINs secret. Change a PIN if someone may have seen it.
  • Open and close the till every day and count the cash honestly. Over / short is how you spot problems early.
  • Keep the backup copy folder (USB or drive) plugged in, and check on the Data page once a week that backups are being made.
  • Take the USB home now and then, so a fire or theft doesn't take both copies.
  • Use a UPS on the main PC. Power cuts in the middle of saving can damage data.
  • Turn the main PC off properly, and leave it on during trading hours (cashier PCs need it).
  • Keep the install files (karots-setup, install.conf) and your backups private. They hold your shop's keys.
  • When something goes wrong, tell us what you did, the receipt number and a photo or screenshot. It makes the fix faster.
  • Install updates from Setup → Data → Updates when we tell you one is ready.

9 · Shop rules: please don't

  • Don't copy, share, resell or give Karots Suite to another shop. It is licensed to your shop only.
  • Don't change the PC's date or time. Receipts, reports and support codes depend on it.
  • Don't touch the database or the data folder directly, or let anyone else do it. Fixing that is charged.
  • Don't install other programs on the till PC, and don't use it for browsing, games or downloads.
  • Don't share the owner's login with staff. Give them a cashier or manager login with the rights they need.
  • Don't run two copies of the shop, or restore an old backup over today's sales without asking us first.
  • Don't let anyone you don't trust use remote access to the PC. We only connect when you ask.

10 · What we promise

  • We never sell or look through your shop's data. It's yours (Terms 6 and Schedule B).
  • We never update your shop without you pressing Update.
  • We never stop your till from selling, even if a payment is late.
  • We quote before any paid work, and we answer within on working days.
