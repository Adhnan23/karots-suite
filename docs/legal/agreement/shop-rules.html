2 · Support and help

Free in the first 3 months and included in the service plan, when done by phone, WhatsApp or a remote session (RustDesk or similar, only when you let us in):

  • How-to questions and help with problems.
  • Setting the shop up your way: categories, menus, prices, receipts, staff and how you work.
  • Bringing your data back from a backup (yours or Karots cloud).
  • Resetting the owner's PIN when every admin is locked out.
  • Setting up a receipt or label printer, barcode scanner or cash drawer.
  • Training a new cashier or manager.

Without a plan, this help is charged by the hour (section 15). Moving to a replacement PC is free in every case (section 9). If you ask us to come to the shop, it's a visit, charged by distance.

3 · Bugs are always fixed free

A genuine bug is Karots Suite doing something wrong that its guide says it does right, on a PC it supports. We fix it free, for as long as you use Karots Suite, with or without a plan. The fix reaches you as an update you install from the Data page. Not bugs: a problem the shop caused (section 4), a fault in the PC, printer or network, wrong data typed in, or something Karots Suite was never made to do.

4 · Always charged

  • Problems the shop caused: deleted data, a virus, settings changed by someone, damage from power cuts. We fix them at the normal rate.
  • Large new features or work made only for your shop.
  • Putting in your products (typing them in or bringing them from another system), unless agreed on the Order Details.
  • Hardware: computers, printers, scanners, drawers, UPS, internet. We can advise, but repairs and parts are not ours, except the hardware we supply with Complete Shop in its first 6 months (section 6).
  • Visits to the shop, by distance.
  • Support without a plan after the first 3 months.

Paid work is quoted before we start, and paid when the job is done unless the quote says otherwise.

5 · The service plan

  • Rs. 5,000 a month or Rs. 60,000 a year, paid in advance, starting after your first 3 months.
  • It brings support and help (section 2), major upgrades, small changes made for your shop, ongoing maintenance, and Karots cloud backup: a copy of your newest backup sent to Karots' cloud about once a day, encrypted with a key for your shop only.
  • If a payment is more than 7 days late, the plan is paused: no new cloud copies, and help is charged. Pay, and it starts again.
  • When the plan ends, new cloud copies stop. The last copy stays for 30 days, then it is deleted; ask us in that time and we bring it back. Your till never stops, and the backups on your own PC and USB carry on.
  • Join again at any time: there's nothing to pay for the months you were away.
  • You can switch cloud backup off yourself on the Data page. Months already paid are not refunded.

9 · One licence, one PC

  • A licence is for the one PC that runs Karots Suite at your shop (the main PC). Cashier PCs and phones that only connect to it need no licence of their own.
  • Running Karots Suite on a second PC at the same time, or at a second shop, needs its own licence.
  • A new PC, or the same PC after a format: tell us, and we move or reinstall Karots Suite free, from your latest backup. We arrange it with you; the old PC stops running it.
  • The shop is sold: tell us in writing. The licence moves to the new owner for a transfer fee of Rs. 5,000, and the new owner signs a new agreement. The 3 months and the refund days carry on from the original installation.
  • The shop moves to new premises: moving Karots Suite is free if we do it remotely, or the visit fee if we come.

12 · Shop rules: please do

  • Give every person their own login and keep PINs secret. Change a PIN if someone may have seen it.
  • Open and close the till every day and count the cash honestly. Over / short is how you spot problems early.
  • Keep the backup copy folder (USB or drive) plugged in, and check on the Data page once a week that backups are being made.
  • Take the USB home now and then, so a fire or theft doesn't take both copies.
  • Use a UPS on the main PC. Power cuts in the middle of saving can damage data.
  • Turn the main PC off properly, and leave it on during trading hours (cashier PCs need it).
  • Keep the install files (karots-setup, install.conf) and your backups private. They hold your shop's keys.
  • When something goes wrong, tell us what you did, the receipt number and a photo or screenshot. It makes the fix faster.
  • Install updates from Setup → Data → Updates when we tell you one is ready.

13 · Shop rules: please don't

  • Don't copy, share, resell or give Karots Suite to another shop. It is licensed to your shop only.
  • Don't change the PC's date or time. Receipts, reports and support codes depend on it.
  • Don't touch the database or the data folder directly, or let anyone else do it. Fixing that is charged.
  • Don't install other programs on the till PC, and don't use it for browsing, games or downloads.
  • Don't share the owner's login with staff. Give them a cashier or manager login with the rights they need.
  • Don't run two copies of the shop, or restore an old backup over today's sales without asking us first.
  • Don't let anyone you don't trust use remote access to the PC. We only connect when you ask.
