## Shared design

- **API:** `/api/v1/admin/*` (`RequireRole(admin, manager)`), as in M2/M3. Lists use `Page[T]`
  with `?offset=` and the "Show older" / `MoreRows` idiom from `features/counter`. Ranges use
  `reports.ResolveRange` (Go) and `features/counter/range.ts` + `RangePicker` (SPA). When a range
  total matters it comes from SQL over the whole range, never from summing a page.
- **Money only through `cashflow.Move/MoveTx`.**
  - A domain row and its move commit in one `appdb.WithTx`.
  - The overdraw guard is `cashflow.guardSource`; never add a second one.
  - Every move response carries `receiptId`, and the form toasts **Print receipt** →
    `printMoneySlip(id)`, as the M3 pay forms do.
  - A move whose source or destination is a till kicks the drawer (the counter's `kickIfTill`
    equivalent).
- **Money-direction cue** on every money button: ↓ green = into a place of ours, ↑ red = out.
- **Picker:** `MoneySourcePicker` + `GET /admin/money-sources` + `parseLocation` (M3) for every
  "paid from / put into" choice. Lockers-only pickers (transfer, settle) filter the same list by
  kind.
- **Charts:** add `echarts` (lazy, `web/src/components/charts/Chart.tsx`, themed from CSS tokens,
  loaded only on chart routes). The Finance hub is its first user and M5 reuses it.
- **Prints:** `/print/cashflow?…` as a React print route (paper/ink tokens). Thermal receipts stay
  in Go (`internal/escpos`).
- **Plugin seams already in `internal/plugin`:**
  - `PLIncome` feeds Finance's income lines.
  - `MoneyLocationProvider` feeds Cash flow's balances and net position.
  - Both render nothing in a core-only build. Core never names a plugin.
- **Legacy cleanup:** delete `expenses.RegisterAPI` / `denominations.RegisterAPI` (+ their
  `APIHandler`) once their admin routes land.

## Upgrades over karots-pos (flaws found while planning, not copied)

All four were fixed in karots-pos first (user, 2026-10-05): `9d1bccd` receivables vs advances,
`d66ac18` expense void, `9f17be1` cash-flow totals + own-place moves. The suite ports those fixes,
and its own `ledger.go` / `voidExpenseTx` / `ExpenseOwner` are the reference.

1. **Customer advances were netted into receivables.**
   - `reports.Compute` and the cashflow net position both use `SUM(customers.outstanding_balance)`,
     so an advance (a negative balance) hid real dues.
   - The suppliers fix of M3 already split payables from supplier credit.
   - This is a domain bug, so it is fixed here **and** in karots-pos as a bug-fix-only commit
     (task 0).
2. **Deleting or editing an expense never moved money back.**
   - The locker or till stayed debited, and an edited amount disagreed with the cash that left.
   - In the suite, an expense's amount and source are locked once paid.
   - **Void** puts the money back into a picked place (pre-filled with the original locker, found
     through `locker_ledger.ref_kind='expense'`) with its own CR- receipt (`expense_void`). The
     expense row is then deleted.
   - A plugin record that owns an expense (`expense_id` in recharge / Print & Copy) blocks the
     void through a new `plugin.ExpenseOwner` hook. The suite adds that hook in task 3; M7
     plugins register on it.
3. **Cash-flow totals came from the newest 500 rows.**
   - Cash in / out / net were summed from a `LIMIT 500` slice, so a busy month under-reported.
   - Now: SQL sums over the whole range, and the ledger pages 50 at a time.
   - Moves **between the shop's own places** (locker↔locker, till↔locker) are left out of in/out
     and shown as "Moved between your places", so a transfer no longer counts once in and once out.
   - "Cash sales at the tills" counts from payments (net of change), the way the drawer does: once
     moves between your own places are left out, till takings would otherwise never count as
     money in.
4. **Unpaged lists:** till sessions (`RecentSessions(100)`) and expenses (all-time) now page. The
   expenses list defaults to this month.

## Review Focus

The five input classes or failure modes most likely to bite a real user. Each one is pinned by a
test in the owning task:

1. **Overdraw and double-submit on a money move.** A transfer, adjust-down, settle, expense or
   void bigger than a non-overdraft locker → 409, with nothing written (no ledger row, expense or
   receipt). A second identical click must not move twice; the form disables while pending.
   Tests: tasks 2, 3.
2. **A till that closed between the form opening and submit.** The picked `till:<id>` has no open
   session → 422 "that till is closed", with nothing written. Tests: tasks 2, 3, 7.
3. **Voiding an old expense whose source can't take money back.** The till session has closed, or
   the locker is archived: no silent fallback. The picker must ask, and an archived locker is
   refused. Test: task 3.
4. **Ranges at the edges.** "Today" at 23:59 and a custom `to` date are inclusive of that whole
   day ([from, to+1d)). An empty range shows zeros, not errors. Cash-flow totals must equal the
   sum of every ledger row in range, across pages. Test: task 4.
5. **Money strings.** Amounts like `"1,500"`, `" 2000 "`, `"0"`, `"-5"` and `"12.345"` → a
   trimmed decimal, rejected when ≤ 0 or above 2 dp, never a float. Settle fee ≥ gross → 422.
   Tests: `parseAmount` table test in task 2, reused by every money handler.
