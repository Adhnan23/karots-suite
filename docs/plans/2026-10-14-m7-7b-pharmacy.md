## Global constraints

- Core never names a plugin; `make check-core` after every task; plugin lists committed core-only (`make plugins P=all`
  to develop, `make core-only` before committing).
- Pharmacy writes only `pharmacy_*`; reads core, Product Plus and Alternatives tables read-only.
- Never stop a sale: every pharmacy check at the till is a hint; recording a dispense is best-effort after the sale.
- karots-pos migrations byte for byte; an old pharmacy backup restores without mapping.
- Theme tokens only; admin works at 360px; files ≤ 500 lines; no AI attribution.
