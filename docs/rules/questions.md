# Questions for the user

Squares in the grid marked **?** — only the shop owner's way of working can answer them. Each says what the suite does
**today**. Answered ones move into the rule and leave this list.

## Area 1 — sales and returns

- **Q1 (RET-16) Card or online sale, returned.** Today the refund is always **cash from the drawer**; the card / bank
  account keeps the money. Is that how shops refund a card sale, or should it go back the way it came (the account
  goes down, the drawer doesn't)?
- **Q2 (RET-17) Cheque sale, returned before the cheque clears.** Today the customer gets **cash at once**, though the
  cheque may still bounce. Refund at once, or only once the cheque has cleared (or cancel the cheque instead)?
- **Q3 (SALE-35) Tax with a bill discount** (tax-registered shops only). Today tax is worked out on each line
  **before** the bill discount, so a bill discount doesn't lower the tax. Is that right, or should the bill discount
  lower the tax too?
- **Q4 (RET-19) Credit sale already paid off, then returned.** A customer buys Rs 1,000 on account, pays the Rs 1,000
  later, then returns the goods. Today the Rs 1,000 **lowers their balance** (they have Rs 1,000 credit with the
  shop), no cash. Should they get cash back instead, or should the cashier choose?
- **Q5 (RET-13) Returning part of a group** (a combo, or a job with its add-ons). Today any line of a group can come
  back on its own, refunded at its share. Allow it, or only the whole group?
