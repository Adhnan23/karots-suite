## Area 3 — customers

- **Q1 (Opening adjusted, Faults)** A customer holds an advance (say Rs 500 of kept change) and the admin then sets an
  old debt of Rs 1,000. **Today** both stand side by side (balance 500, old debt 1,000), and the next payment quietly
  shrinks the old debt to match the balance, so the old-debt figure jumps. *Suggested:* the advance pays the old debt
  at once (old debt 500, balance 500); the old debt is never more than what they owe.
- **Q2 (Payment, Faults)** Collect credit sent twice (a double tap, or Pay pressed again after the answer was lost).
  **Today** two payments are recorded, two DP- slips print and the drawer expects both. *Suggested:* the payment
  carries a one-time reference like the till's Pay (SALE-50), so the second gets the first back.
- **Q3 (Disabled customer, Limit)** A sale on account to a disabled customer (a held sale or a screen opened before
  they were disabled). **Today** it goes through like any other. *Suggested:* it goes through (never stop a sale) and
  is flagged for review, "customer disabled".
